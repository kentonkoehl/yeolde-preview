Ordering Guide

We do our best to keep it simple. Choose an entree, then choose one side to go with your house salad. Every entree comes with hot homemade bread. Want to ditch the salad for another side? That’s a $2 charge. Super simple.

With the exception of the Broccoli Casserole, desserts, and the fried options, menu items are gluten free. Please tell your server about any food allergies or dietary restrictions. Consuming raw or undercooked meat or eggs may increase the risk of food-borne illness.
